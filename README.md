# NovaStack-AstroHealth.
AstroHealth is an astronaut health monitoring system designed for space missions. It provides a dashboard to monitor health indicators, visualize health trends, and generate alerts using NASA-related research and simulated health data.

Project Title: Health Guardian - Health Monitoring Software for Astronauts

Problem Statement:
Astronauts in space face risks like abnormal heart rate, low oxygen, fatigue, stress, dehydration and harmful space environment. There is no proper system to monitor all these together in real-time. Our software Health Guardian tracks astronaut health live, predicts risks with AI, and alerts Mission Control in emergency.

Unique Features:
1. Health Monitoring - Heart Rate, Oxygen, Temperature
2. AI Risk Predictor - Predicts health risks
3. Fatigue Detector - Sleep and fatigue tracking
4. Space Environment - Temperature, Humidity, CO2
5. Emergency Alert - Instant alert on health issue
6. AI Health Assistant - Explains health records
7. Ground Control - Reports for medical team on Earth
